Bio

My research starts from a question that looks simple but sits at the heart of modern computing: how do you keep a large-scale cluster busy without keeping users waiting? I design scheduling policies that are simple enough to deploy in production, yet strong enough to approach optimal performance — and I validate them against real workloads from Google Borg and Alibaba. This line of work has recently extended to AI inference serving and connected-vehicle networks.

Research Interests

Cluster Scheduling & Resource Allocation

Designing dispatching and scheduling policies for data centers and HPC systems that keep machines busy while minimizing user waiting times, validated on Google Borg and Alibaba production workloads.

Energy–Delay Trade-offs

Jointly optimizing a cluster's energy efficiency and response time in multiserver systems — without requiring prior knowledge of job sizes.

Job Runtime Prediction

Machine-learning models that predict how long HPC jobs will take, so the scheduler can place them well before they start.

LLM Inference Serving

How multiple large language models share a single GPU server — offloading, preemption, and cache migration to keep latency low for all of them.

Vehicular Networks & 6G

The timeliness of safety-critical information in V2X communication — Age of Information and delay metrics for connected vehicles as networks evolve toward 6G.
